# SAHAYA — Cooperative Gig Services Platform


**Domain:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development  
**Problem Statement:** Cooperative Gig Services Platform for Household & Community Services

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Labour Cooperative Federations and Labour Cooperative Societies possess a large pool of skilled workers, including electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, caregivers, and technicians. However, these workers lack a structured digital platform to connect with households and institutions requiring their services.

Limited digital visibility, lack of digitally verifiable trust, commission-heavy platforms, inadequate welfare support, and limited smartphone access create significant challenges for cooperative workers.

## 2. Proposed Solution

SAHAYA is a cooperative-owned digital platform that connects verified cooperative workers with customers through a unified service-booking system.

The platform integrates worker verification, location-based matching, service booking, transparent pricing, AI-based demand forecasting, digital payments, and worker welfare support.

It provides consumer and worker applications, an administrative dashboard, and an IVR-based access system for users without smartphones.

## 3. Objectives

- Digitize cooperative worker records and skills.
- Connect verified workers with customers.
- Promote fair earnings through a zero-worker-commission model.
- Enable transparent service booking and pricing.
- Improve worker allocation using location and availability.
- Provide IVR access for users without smartphones.
- Introduce AI-based service-demand forecasting.
- Support worker welfare through SAHAYA Shield.

## 4. Target Users

### Workers
- Electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, caregivers, cleaners, drivers, and technicians.
- Workers belonging to Labour Cooperative Federations and Labour Cooperative Societies.
- Workers with limited smartphone access or digital literacy.

### Customers
- Households requiring domestic and maintenance services.
- Businesses and institutions requiring skilled workers.
- Customers seeking verified, reliable, and affordable local services.

## 5. Key Features

- **Verified Worker Profiles:** Cooperative-based worker registration and verification.
- **Service Booking:** Easy booking and job-status tracking.
- **Location-Based Matching:** Connects customers with suitable nearby workers.
- **Transparent Pricing:** Displays service costs and worker earnings clearly.
- **IVR-Based Access:** Enables service requests without requiring a smartphone application.
- **AI-Based Demand Forecasting:** Predicts service demand across locations.
- **Intelligent Worker Allocation:** Considers skills, availability, location, and workload.
- **Digital Payments:** Supports secure online transactions through an integrated payment provider.
- **Administrative Dashboard:** Enables worker management, booking monitoring, and complaint resolution.
- **SAHAYA Shield:** Proposed worker welfare initiative for eligible workers.

## 6. Worker Registration and Verification

1. Workers register through participating cooperative societies or federations.
2. Authorized cooperative personnel verify membership and relevant details.
3. Worker skills, experience, and service areas are recorded.
4. Approved workers receive verified digital profiles.
5. Verified workers become eligible for suitable service requests.
6. Workers can manage availability and track assigned jobs.

## 7. System Workflow

1. **Worker Registration:** Workers are onboarded through cooperative federations.
2. **Verification:** Worker credentials and skills are verified by authorized personnel.
3. **Service Request:** Customers select a service and provide their location.
4. **Worker Matching:** The system identifies suitable workers based on skills, availability, and location.
5. **Booking Confirmation:** A suitable worker accepts the request.
6. **Service Execution:** The worker completes the requested service.
7. **Payment and Feedback:** Payment is recorded, and customers can provide feedback.
8. **AI-Based Analytics:** Booking data supports demand forecasting and workforce planning.

## 9. SAHAYA Shield — Worker Welfare

SAHAYA Shield is a proposed worker welfare initiative designed to provide structured support to eligible cooperative workers.

### Objectives
- Facilitate emergency assistance.
- Support eligible workers through welfare initiatives.
- Maintain transparent welfare-request and approval processes.
- Improve access to information about available welfare schemes.
- Promote accountability through documented contributions and disbursements.
